# 写作与翻译专项

> 本文是《六级备考规划（2026年12月）》系列之一，返回 [README](/19-六级备考/)。
> 配套阅读：[02-分阶段总体规划](/19-六级备考/02-分阶段总体规划)（9周日历与每日时间分配）、[06-单词计划](/19-六级备考/06-单词计划)、[07-资料与工具清单](/19-六级备考/07-资料与工具清单)、[08-每日打卡与每周复盘表](/19-六级备考/08-每日打卡与每周复盘表)。
> 本专项目标：写译合计从四级 146/213 提升到 **150–155/213**，定位"不拖后腿即可"。每天投入写译 10–15 分钟，周末集中练；9 周内背熟三套作文模板、四类话题词表（共约 145 条）和 15 个翻译高频句型。

### 怎么用本文件（9 周使用顺序）

第一阶段（W1–W3，基础期）只做两件事：把 1.3 的三套模板骨架和 1.4 的 24 句功能句读到能默写，把 2.4 的四类词表按周拆开背。这个阶段不写整篇作文，目标是"输入到位"，否则后面仿写会边写边翻模板，考试时卡死。

第二阶段（W4–W7，强化期）开始按 1.5 和 2.6 的周历做真题仿写/翻译精练，写完必须用 1.6 的 AI 批改 prompt 过一遍，把 AI 指出的错误抄进错题本。**只写不改等于白写**——六级写译丢分大头不是词汇量不够，而是主谓不一致、时态混乱这类重复低级错误。

第三阶段（W8–W9，冲刺期）不再开新题，任务是默写模板、重做错题篇、跟一遍"我是瑞斯拜"考前预测。考场上写作和翻译都属于"套模板 + 不犯错"就能拿中等偏上分数的部分，不需要临场发挥。

## 一、写作部分

### 1.1 考情与评分档次

| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 时间 | 15:10–15:40，**全卷第一个考，30 分钟**，写在答题卡 1 上 |
| 字数 | **150–200 词**（少于 130 词明显扣分） |
| 分值 | 占全卷 15%，报道分约 106.5（与翻译合计 213） |
| 题型 | 短文写作 1 题，议论文/观点评论为主 |

评分按**满分 15 分五档**给分（公开阅卷口径）：

| 档次 | 大致标准 |
|---|---|
| 13–15 分 | 切题，思想表达清楚，结构清晰，文字连贯，基本无语言错误 |
| 10–12 分 | 切题，思想表达清楚，文字较连贯，有少量语言错误 |
| 7–9 分 | 基本切题，思想不够清楚，连贯性差，有较多严重语言错误 |
| 4–6 分 | 基本不切题，思想不清，连贯性差，语言错误多且严重 |
| 1–3 分 | 不切题，条理不清，语言支离破碎 |

对本考生的含义：目标只需稳定落在 **10–12 分档**，即"切题 + 三段结构清楚 + 主谓一致/时态不犯错"，不需要堆难词。下面三套模板就是为这一档设计的。

### 1.3 三套可直接背诵的模板骨架（核心交付物）

#### 模板一：观点对比 / 利弊讨论型

适用：题目给出两种看法，或讨论某现象的利弊（如"全球化下还要不要文化自信""大学生用 AI 工具好不好"）。

三段式结构：
- 第一段（2–3 句）：引入现象 + 转述两方观点
- 第二段（4–6 句）：正方 1 个论证点 + 反方 1 个论证点，各带 1 句小结
- 第三段（2–3 句）：给出"我的看法" + 一句折中/展望

英文骨架：

```
Nowadays, [Topic] has become a heated topic among college students.
Some people argue that [View A]. Others, however, hold that [View B].

Those in favor of [View A] believe that [Reason 1]. They often point out
that [Example 1]. Yet the other side argues that [Reason 2]. Moreover,
[Reason 3], which clearly shows the value of [View B].

From my perspective, [My Opinion]. We can [Compromise]. Only in this way
can [Expected Result].
```

中文填充指引：
- [Topic]：照抄题目名词短语，如 cultural confidence / using AI tools
- [View A]/[View B]：用简单句写出题目里那两句话，**不要直接抄题干原句，换个说法**
- [Reason 1–3]：一个写个人/学习，一个写社会/国家，两句话内说完，不展开长例子
- [My Opinion]：默认写"折中"，即"两方都有道理，但我更偏 B"，最稳妥

**示范作文（套 2026.6 第二套"文化自信"，约 175 词）**：

Nowadays, cultural confidence has become a heated topic among college students. Some people argue that, in an increasingly globalized world, Chinese traditional culture is being replaced by Western lifestyles and is no longer practical. Others, however, hold that young people should take pride in their own cultural roots.

Those in favor of foreign lifestyles believe that Western culture is more fashionable and efficient in daily life. They tend to ignore traditional festivals and classic works, and seldom attend exhibitions of Chinese art. Yet the other side argues that a nation without its own culture is like a tree without roots. Moreover, rich traditions such as calligraphy, Peking Opera and herbal medicine have won growing admiration across the world.

From my perspective, cultural confidence does not mean rejecting everything foreign. We can absorb the best of other cultures while proudly carrying forward our own heritage. Only in this way can young students achieve a truly open and balanced mind.

#### 模板二：现象解释 / 问题解决型

适用：题目描述一种社会现象（"现在越来越多年轻人……""大学生面对挑战"），要求分析原因并给出建议。

三段式结构：
- 第一段（2–3 句）：描述现象现状
- 第二段（4–6 句）：2 个原因，每个原因 1–2 句，最后 1 句说后果
- 第三段（2–3 句）：2 个解决办法 + 一句升华

英文骨架：

```
In recent years, [Phenomenon] has become common among [Target Group].
[One-sentence description of the phenomenon].

This trend has two main causes. First, [Reason 1]. Second, [Reason 2].
As a result, [Consequence].

To solve this problem, practical measures should be taken. [Institution]
can [Measure 1], and students ourselves should [Measure 2]. [Benefit].
It is high time we [Call to action].
```

中文填充指引：
- [Phenomenon]：用名词短语写题目描述的现象，如 young people growing apart from nature
- [Reason 1] 写学业/工作压力，[Reason 2] 写手机/网络诱惑，是最万能的两个原因
- [Measure 1] 写给学校/社会，[Measure 2] 写给学生自己，各一句
- "It is high time we + 动词过去式"是结尾万能句型，务必背熟

**示范作文（套 2026.6 第一套"热爱自然"，约 175 词）**：

In recent years, many young people in cities have grown apart from nature. They spend most of their time indoors, buried in textbooks or smartphones, and seldom step into parks, forests or mountains.

This trend has two main causes. First, the fast pace of study and work leaves college students little spare time for outdoor activities. Second, attractive online entertainment such as short videos and games keeps them sitting at the screen for hours. As a result, more and more young people suffer from stress, poor eyesight and weak bodies, and some even complain about constant anxiety.

To solve this problem, practical measures should be taken. Universities can organize regular hiking and camping trips, and students themselves should set aside at least one afternoon a week to walk in the open air. Getting close to nature not only builds up our health but also calms our minds. It is high time we reconnected with the natural world.

#### 模板三：名言 / 观点评论型（含给定开头句续写）

适用：题目直接给一句名言或一段开头句（2025.12 第三套、2026.6 第三套都是这种），要求评论展开。

三段式结构：
- 第一段（2–3 句）：**照抄给定开头句**，然后用 1–2 句解释这句话的含义
- 第二段（4–6 句）：用 For one thing… / For another… 给两个论证点
- 第三段（2–3 句）：怎么做到（学校/个人各一句）+ 一句总结

英文骨架：

```
[Given opening sentence — copied exactly as it appears]. [One-sentence
interpretation: as the future builders of our country, we are expected
to explain what it really means in our daily life].

This quality/idea matters for at least two reasons. For one thing,
[Reason 1 + its effect]. For another, [Reason 2 + its effect].

To develop this spirit, universities should [Measure 1], and students
should start from small daily acts, such as [Example 1] and [Example 2].
[Key concept] is not a slogan; it is the key that enables young people
to grow into reliable adults.
```

中文填充指引：
- **给定开头句必须一字不差照抄**，然后接一句解释
- For one thing 写"对个人成长好"，For another 写"对社会好"，两个点不重叠
- 结尾"…is not a slogan; it is the key…"是本模板的标志性收束句

**示范作文（套 2026.6 第三套"青年责任"给定开头句续写，约 185 词）**：

In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people's sense of responsibility. As the future builders of our country, college students are expected to take responsibility not only for their own growth but also for society as a whole.

A strong sense of responsibility matters for at least two reasons. For one thing, responsible young people are more likely to work hard, respect others and behave honestly, which lays a solid foundation for their future careers. For another, when every young person shoulders his or her duties, such as protecting the environment and helping people in need, the whole society will become warmer and more harmonious.

To cultivate this spirit, universities should offer more volunteer programs, and students should start from small daily acts, such as finishing tasks on time and caring for family members. Responsibility is not a slogan; it is the key that enables young people to grow into reliable adults.

### 1.4 万能功能句库（24 句，选稳妥句型）

开头引入：
1. Nowadays, … has become a heated topic among college students.（如今……已成为大学生热议的话题。）
2. In recent years, … has become increasingly common.（近年来……越来越普遍。）
3. With the rapid development of society, great changes have taken place in…（随着社会快速发展，……发生了巨大变化。）
4. When it comes to …, people's opinions vary from person to person.（谈到……，人们的观点因人而异。）

表达观点：
5. From my perspective, …（在我看来，……）
6. As far as I am concerned, …（就我而言，……）
7. I am firmly convinced that …（我坚信……）
8. My reasons can be listed as follows.（我的理由如下。）

递进 / 举例：
9. For one thing, … For another, …（一方面……另一方面……）
10. Moreover, / Furthermore, …（此外，……）
11. Take … as an example.（以……为例。）
12. A good case in point is …（一个很好的例子是……）

转折 / 让步：
13. However, every coin has two sides.（然而，凡事都有两面性。）
14. Although … has its advantages, it also brings problems.（虽然……有好处，但也带来问题。）
15. Some people argue that …, but I hold a different view.（有人认为……，但我持不同看法。）
16. Despite these problems, … still plays an important role.（尽管有这些问题，……仍起着重要作用。）

因果：
17. As a result, …（结果，……）
18. Thanks to …, we can now …（多亏了……，我们现在能够……）
19. The main reason why … is that …（……的主要原因是……）
20. Therefore, effective measures should be taken immediately.（因此，应立即采取有效措施。）

总结呼吁：
21. In conclusion, we should attach great importance to …（总之，我们应高度重视……）
22. It is high time we took action to …（是我们采取行动……的时候了。）
23. Only in this way can we …（只有这样我们才能……。倒装句，背熟就会用）
24. Let us work together to make … a better place.（让我们共同努力，把……建设得更好。）

### 1.6 批改闭环：写完怎么改

**首选：国产 AI 大模型免费批改**（豆包 doubao.com / DeepSeek chat.deepseek.com / Kimi kimi.moonshot.cn 网页版日常免费）。直接复制下面这段 prompt 用：

```
你是一位大学英语六级阅卷老师，按以下步骤批改我的作文：

题目：[粘贴六级作文题目]
我的作文：[粘贴你的英文作文]

第一步 硬性检查：
- 实际字数（给出数字）
- 是否覆盖题目要求的每个要点
- 书信题：称呼与落款格式是否正确

第二步 定档：
- 判断这篇作文落在哪一档（13-15分/10-12分/7-9分/4-6分），档内偏上还是偏下，说明依据

第三步 逐句批改：
- 列出所有语法错误、用词不当、中式英语表达
- 给出修改建议

第四步 升格示范：
- 挑选3-5个平淡句子，改写成更高级的表达（用从句、非谓语、高级词汇）

第五步 总结：
- 我的主要问题是什么
- 下次写作需要注意的3个重点
```

### 1.7 考场 30 分钟操作表

| 时间 | 动作 |
|---|---|
| 0–3 分钟 | 审题：判断题型（对比/现象/评论），在草稿纸上写 3 个中文词——[Topic]、两个 [Reason]、我的结论 |
| 3–23 分钟（约 20 分钟） | 套用对应模板写正文，每段写完空一行或缩进 4 个字母格 |
| 23–28 分钟 | 检查：主谓一致、时态、可数名词单复数、拼写、字数是否 150–200 |
| 28–30 分钟 | 补漏：字数不够在主体段加一句举例；不够顺的词换简单词，**不要整段涂改** |

卷面要求：黑色签字笔书写；写错一道斜线划掉即可，不涂黑团；每段缩进或段间空行，方便阅卷老师一眼看出三段结构。

### 1.8 常见低级错误清单（错 → 对）

| 错误类型 | 错句 | 正确 |
|---|---|---|
| 主谓不一致 | Everyone have their own dream. | Everyone **has** their own dream. |
| 时态混乱（全文一会儿现在一会儿过去） | Now young people spent less time outdoors. | Now young people **spend** less time outdoors. |
| 可数名词裸奔（单数可数名词前无冠词） | Student should take responsibility. | **A** student / **Students** should take responsibility. |
| 逗号连接两个句子（comma splice） | Nature is important, it calms our mind. | Nature is important**, for** it calms our mind. / Nature is important**. It** calms our mind. |
| 中式英语搭配 | open our eyes to the world（chinglish 直译"开阔眼界"可用，但更稳的是） | broaden our horizons |
| 中式英语搭配 | learn knowledge | **acquire** knowledge |
| 中式英语搭配 | very like | **really** like / are fond of |
| there be 与 have 混用 | There have many challenges. | There **are** many challenges. |

### 2.3 翻译四步法操作卡

**第一步：通读断句。** 把 180–200 汉字按语义切成 3–5 个意群，中文长句不要一句硬译到底。

**第二步：找主干。** 每个意群先找"谁 + 做什么"，主谓宾先译出来，修饰成分后补。

**第三步：处理修饰成分。** 中文定语长就后置成 which/that 从句或 with 短语；中文无主语句补 we/people 或直接用被动；状语（时间/地点/方式）放句首或句尾。

**第四步：回读检查。** 时态、单复数、冠词、固定搭配。

演示句（2026.6 无人机真题风格）：

> 原文：近年来，中国无人机产业发展迅速，产品出口到许多国家。

- 断句：近年来 / 中国无人机产业发展迅速 / 产品出口到许多国家
- 主干：产业发展迅速；产品出口
- 成句：**In recent years, China's drone industry has developed rapidly, and its products are exported to many countries.**
- 检查：has developed（现在完成时配 in recent years）✓；are exported（被动，无主语句）✓；products 复数 ✓

### 2.5 翻译高频句型库（15 个）

1. **随着……的发展**：With the rapid development of… — With the rapid development of e-commerce, rural farmers can now sell their products nationwide.（随着电商的快速发展，农民可以把产品卖到全国。）
2. **……在……中起着重要作用**：…plays an important role in… — Drones play an important role in agricultural plant protection.（无人机在农业植保中起着重要作用。）
3. **不仅……而且**：not only…but also… — Telemedicine not only saves patients' time but also improves the quality of diagnosis.（远程医疗不仅节省患者时间，还提高诊断质量。）
4. **据说/据报道**：It is said / reported that… — It is reported that China exports the largest number of civilian drones in the world.（据报道，中国民用无人机出口量全球第一。）
5. **被动句表泛指**：sth. be done… — Online medical consultation platforms are widely used in remote areas.（在线医疗咨询平台在偏远地区被广泛使用。）
6. **无主语句处理**：We should… / People are encouraged to… — We should attach importance to the protection of cultural heritage.（我们应重视文化遗产保护。）
7. **定语从句合并句**：…, which… — The Mogao Grottoes, which date back to the 4th century, attract millions of visitors every year.（莫高窟始建于 4 世纪，每年吸引数百万游客。）
8. **使……成为可能**：make it possible (for sb.) to do sth. — Mobile payment makes it possible for villagers to buy anything at home.（移动支付让村民足不出户就能买到任何东西。）
9. **……的数量达到**：The number of… has reached… — The number of 5G users in China has reached one billion.（中国 5G 用户数量已达到 10 亿。）
10. **致力于 / 努力做**：be committed to / strive to do… — China is committed to narrowing the urban-rural gap.（中国致力于缩小城乡差距。）
11. **自从……以来**：It has been… years since… — It has been over twenty years since China launched its first manned spacecraft.（中国发射第一艘载人飞船至今已有二十多年。）
12. **被认为是**：be regarded as / be considered as — The Belt and Road Initiative is regarded as a platform for win-win cooperation.（"一带一路"被视为合作共赢的平台。）
13. **为了满足……的需要**：in order to meet the needs of… — In order to meet the needs of elderly patients, more community clinics have been built.（为满足老年患者的需求，更多社区诊所被建起。）
14. **与……相比**：compared with… — Compared with traditional farming, drone spraying saves up to 90 percent of pesticides.（与传统耕作相比，无人机喷洒可节省多达 90% 的农药。）
15. **因此 / 由此可见**：Therefore, / As a result, … — Livestreaming breaks the information barrier between cities and villages. As a result, farmers' income has risen steadily.（直播打破了城乡信息壁垒，因此农民收入稳步增长。）

### 2.7 考场 30 分钟操作表

| 时间 | 动作 |
|---|---|
| 0–5 分钟 | 通读全文断句，圈出不会的词（先标记，不卡住） |
| 5–25 分钟 | 按四步法逐句翻译，先写主谓宾再补修饰；不会的词立刻绕译 |
| 25–30 分钟 | 回读检查时态/单复数/冠词，补全漏译句 |

**不会的词怎么绕**：不许空着。用解释性翻译或同义替换——"植保喷洒农药"不会说 pesticide spraying，就写"use drones to spray crops"；"远程医疗"不会说 telemedicine，就写"medical care provided through the Internet"。意思到了就给分，空着一定 0 分。
